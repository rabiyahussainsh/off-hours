About Off Hours

Off Hours is a coffee brand built for the moments in between — slow mornings, late-night conversations, and everything that happens when the day finally quiets down. We source thoughtfully selected beans from around the world and roast them in small batches to bring out their distinct character, creating coffee that feels as good to drink as it does to share. No rush, no rules — just good coffee, made for your off hours.
